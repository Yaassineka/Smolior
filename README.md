# Smolior, Made by 1 person with LOVE <3

Smolior is an action-packed FPS where tiny warriors wage intense battles. In bomb mode, attackers must plant and protect their objective while defenders fight to stop them. For those who crave nonstop action, deathmatch mode is a chaotic free-for-all where every shot matters. With its fast-paced gameplay and unique small-scale combat, "Smolior" is built with passion and constantly evolving, with more exciting modes on the way!
